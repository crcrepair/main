## What to Look For in Phone Repair Shops Near You

A search for **mobile phone repair shops near me** can return a dozen businesses within a couple of miles. The results you get for **"phone repair shops near me"** vary a lot in quality, so before you hand over your phone, check these six things:

1. **A written warranty.** A decent shop puts a warranty on both parts and labour, usually 3 to 12 months. If they won't put it in writing, go somewhere else.
2. **Clear part grades.** Ask whether the replacement is genuine (OEM), refurbished OEM, or aftermarket. All three have their uses, but you should be told which one you're getting and what it costs.
3. **A free diagnosis.** Good **mobile phone repair shops** check the fault and quote before they start work. Be wary of anyone who charges just to look.
4. **Recent reviews.** Read the latest Google reviews, not only the star rating. Look for mentions of turnaround time, communication and how the shop dealt with problems.
5. **Data privacy.** Most repairs don't need your passcode. If one does, the shop should explain why, and you should back up your phone first.
6. **Same-day service.** Screens and batteries for popular models are usually kept in stock. If a shop needs a week for a common iPhone screen, they probably have to order the part in.

**Quick tip:** Ask *"Will Face ID / True Tone / water resistance still work after the repair?"* A good technician will give you a straight answer for your model.

## Phone Screen Repair: What's Involved

**Phone screen repair** is the job we do most. When people search **phone screen repair near me** or **mobile screen repair near me**, it's usually one of three problems:

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cracked glass, display works fine | Outer glass broken | Screen assembly replacement (glass and display are usually bonded together) |
| Black patches, lines or flickering | Damaged OLED/LCD panel | Full display replacement |
| Screen lights up but won't respond to touch | Damaged digitiser or connector | Display replacement or connector repair |

### Why not just live with the crack?

A cracked screen is more than an eyesore:

- **Glass splinters** can cut your fingers.
- **Moisture and dust** get in through the cracks and can damage the board.
- **Touch faults spread.** A small crack often turns into a dead area of the screen.
- **Trade-in value falls**, sometimes by more than the repair would cost.

### Genuine vs aftermarket screens

On OLED iPhones and Samsung Galaxy phones, the screen grade makes a visible difference. Aftermarket LCD panels cost less but tend to be dimmer, use more battery and look different at an angle. We'll show you both options and the price difference before you decide.

## iPhone Battery Replacement Near Me: Signs You Need One

**iPhone battery replacement near me** is one of the most searched repairs, and with good reason. Lithium-ion batteries wear out, usually after 2–3 years of daily charging.

**Check your battery health:** go to *Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging*. If **Maximum Capacity is below 80%**, the battery is worth replacing.

Other signs:

- The phone turns off at 20–30%.
- It's slow even after a restart (iOS throttles performance when the battery is worn).
- The phone gets hot when charging.
- **The screen is lifting or the case is bulging.** That means the battery is swelling. Stop using the phone and get it seen straight away.

A battery swap usually takes **30–60 minutes**. For a phone that's otherwise fine, it's the cheapest way to get another year or two out of it.

## Tablet Screen Repair Near Me

Tablets break in different ways from phones. They get dropped off sofas, stood on and thrown about by children. A **tablet screen repair** is usually more fiddly than a phone repair because:

- On many iPads the **glass digitiser and the LCD are separate parts**, so sometimes only the glass needs replacing, which is cheaper.
- Tablet screens are held in with **strong adhesive** and have to be heated and lifted off slowly to avoid damaging the display underneath.
- Home button and Touch ID cables are easy to tear, so this needs an experienced technician.

If you're searching **tablet screen repair near me**, ask the shop whether your model has a separate glass part. It can make a big difference to the price. We repair iPads, Samsung Galaxy Tabs, Microsoft Surface and Amazon Fire tablets. See our [tablet repair services](/services/tablet-repair).

## Home Mobile Phone Repairs: Is a Call-Out Worth It?

You don't always have to visit a shop. **Home mobile phone repairs** (a technician comes to your home or workplace) suit you if:

- you can't be without your phone for work,
- you have limited mobility or no easy way to get to a shop,
- you need several devices repaired at once (families, small offices).

**Good for home repair:** screen replacements, battery swaps, charging port cleaning or replacement, camera lens glass.

**Better done in the workshop:** water damage, board-level (micro-soldering) faults, data recovery and Face ID repairs. These need specialist equipment.

<a id="repair-or-replace"></a>
## Repair or Replace?

As a rule of thumb, **if the repair costs less than half the price of an equivalent replacement phone, repair it.** Repairing is usually the better choice when:

- the phone is less than 4 years old and still gets software updates,
- it's only one fault (screen *or* battery),
- you want to keep your photos, apps and settings exactly as they are.

It's also much better for the environment. Making a new smartphone produces far more carbon than repairing an old one.

Think about replacing if the phone no longer gets security updates, has several faults at once, or has board damage that costs more to fix than the phone is worth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find a good phone repair shop near me?
Look for a shop that gives a written warranty, tells you which grade of part it's using, diagnoses for free and has recent positive Google reviews. Ask whether they can do the repair the same day and whether features like Face ID and water resistance will still work afterwards.

### How long does a phone screen repair take?
Most phone screen repairs take 45 minutes to 2 hours if the part is in stock. Less common models may need the part ordered, which usually takes 1–3 working days.

### Is it worth replacing an iPhone battery?
Yes, if the battery's Maximum Capacity is below 80% and the phone is otherwise fine. It's a quick, cheap repair that brings back battery life and speed.

### Do you offer home mobile phone repairs?
Yes. Screens, batteries and charging ports can usually be repaired at your home or workplace. More complex faults such as water damage are done in our workshop.

### Will I lose my data during a repair?
Screen and battery repairs don't normally affect your data. Back up your phone before any repair anyway, just in case.

### Can you repair tablet screens?
Yes. We repair iPad, Samsung Galaxy Tab, Microsoft Surface and Amazon Fire screens, often the same day.
