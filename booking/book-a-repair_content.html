Booking a repair: frequently asked questions

Do I have to book, or can I just walk in?
Walk-ins are welcome at all 10 stores. Booking online is better, though: it reserves the parts for your model, gives you a fixed time slot and usually means a faster turnaround. If you'd rather talk to someone, call 0330 133 1131 or your local store.

My device or fault isn't in the booking list. Can you still fix it?
Usually, yes. The booking tool covers the most common repairs, but our technicians work on older models, less common brands and faults that need a diagnosis first. Book the closest option and describe the problem in the notes, or contact us and we'll quote by phone or email.

Is the price shown online the final price?
The price shown is the price for that repair on that model, with no hidden fees. If the technician finds a different or additional fault once the device is on the bench, we'll call you with a revised quote before doing any work – nothing is done without your go-ahead.

How long does a repair take?
Most phone screen and battery replacements are finished the same day, often within 1–3 hours when the parts are in stock – and booking makes sure they are. Laptop, MacBook, console and board-level repairs can take longer; the store will give you an expected collection time when you drop the device off.

Can I change or cancel my booking?
Yes. Call the store you booked with and we'll move the slot or cancel it. You can also manage quotes and repairs in our Repair Portal.

Do you still repair older iPhones and Samsungs?
In most cases, yes. Screens and batteries for older iPhone and Galaxy models are still available and often cheaper than you'd expect. If your model isn't listed online, call your nearest store and we'll check parts availability for you.

What parts do you use?
Genuine and OEM-quality parts. Genuine Apple parts are available for iPhone, Samsung repairs use genuine Samsung parts and Google Pixel repairs use genuine Google parts. Every device is tested before it's handed back.

What warranty do I get?
A lifetime warranty on phone screen repairs and a 12-month warranty on most other repairs, covering parts and labour. Board-level soldering repairs carry a three-month warranty.

What should I bring to my appointment?
Just the device. Back up your data first if you can, remove the case, and know your passcode in case we need it to test the repair. If the device is on an insurance claim, bring the claim reference.

What if I can't get to a store?
Choose our collection and drop-off service (from £29.95, available from five stores) and we'll collect, repair and return the device, or book a come-to-you repair at your home or workplace.
