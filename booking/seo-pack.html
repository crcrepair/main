3. Technical fixes the data points to

  • Two URLs are indexed: /book-a-repair/ (74,079 impressions, position 61) and /book-a-repair without the slash (817 impressions, position 6, 15 clicks). Make the slash-less one 301 to the slashed one (Settings → Permalinks keeps trailing slashes; check the redirect plugin or .htaccess) and use the slashed URL in every Google Business Profile.
  • Deep-link states (?selected=repairs/Apple/iPhone-15-Pro) can't be indexed as separate pages – that's expected. Keep their canonical on /book-a-repair/; the per-model SEO belongs to the pillar and product pages, which the content links to.
  • Business Profiles: the Top-10 plan recommends pointing each profile at its city page (/romford/ etc.), with this booking page as the "Appointment" link. That sends local searches to the local page and bookings here.
  • Not offered: the page also shows for "tv repair near me" (2,270 impressions) and 3D-printer/drone queries. The new text doesn't mention TVs, so those impressions should fade.
  • "pocket geek" (2,023 impressions here): keep using the full name "Pocket Geek Tech Repair by CrC Repair®", as on the homepage.
