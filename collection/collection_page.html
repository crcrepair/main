Collection and drop-off: frequently asked questions

Is collection and drop-off a same-day service?
It can be, depending on the repair. That's why we book collections in advance: when we call you we check the parts are in stock and plan the slot so the repair is completed in the shortest possible time. Simple repairs such as phone screens and batteries are often back with you the same day; laptop, console and board-level repairs can take longer, and we'll tell you the expected return date when we call.

Is my phone safe?
Yes. Collections and returns are made by our own fully insured staff, not a third-party courier, and your device is covered by our insurance from the moment we collect it until it's back in your hands. In store it's booked into our system and handled only by our technicians, and you can follow its progress in the Repair Portal.

How much does the collection and drop-off service cost?
The basic fee is £29.95, which covers collection and return within 5 miles of the store. Beyond that we charge £1 for each additional mile. We confirm the exact fee on the call before anything is booked, and the repair itself is quoted separately.

Which areas do you cover?
The service runs from our Bournemouth, Farringdon, Surrey Quays, Romford and Whetstone stores. There's no fixed cut-off: give us your postcode on the form and we'll confirm on the call whether we can reach you and what the mileage fee will be.

Which devices can you collect?
Phones (iPhone, Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola and other Android), tablets and iPads, laptops, MacBooks and desktop PCs, and games consoles (PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo Switch).

Do I need to do anything before you collect?
Back up your data if you can, and remove any case, SIM tray tools or accessories you want to keep. If we need your passcode to test the repair, we'll ask for it on the call and you can remove it again when the device is returned.

Is the warranty the same as an in-store repair?
Yes. Collected devices are repaired in our own stores by the same technicians, with the same lifetime warranty on phone screen repairs and a 12-month warranty on most other repairs.

What happens if I don't want to go ahead with the quote?
We always quote before any work starts. If you decide not to proceed, we'll bring your device back to you unrepaired; only the collection fee is payable.

Can businesses use the collection service?
Yes. It's ideal for offices and other organisations with several devices to repair: one collection, one return and a single point of contact. Call us on 0330 133 1131 or email info@crcrepair.co.uk to arrange regular collections.

Can you collect from my workplace instead of my home?
Yes. We can collect from and return to any address we can reach – home, office or elsewhere – and we agree the time slot with you on the call.
