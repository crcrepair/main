Phone, tablet, laptop and console repairs in detail

Screen replacement and cracked screen repair

A cracked or smashed screen is the fault we see most, and screen replacement is what CrC Repair® is known for. We replace OLED and LCD displays on iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel and Motorola phones, on iPads and Android tablets, and on laptops and MacBooks.

Phone screen replacements are usually finished the same day, often within 1–3 hours, and every phone screen we fit is covered by our lifetime warranty. Not sure what a repair should cost? Read our guide to iPhone screen repair costs in the UK and the difference between OLED and LCD screens, then get an instant quote with the booking tool above.

Same-day phone repair near you

Searching for “phone repair near me” or “phone repair shops near me”? With 10 stores from Bournemouth to Glasgow, there's a good chance one is close by. Walk in with a broken phone and our technicians will diagnose it and quote before any work starts. Most screen, battery, charging-port, camera, microphone and speaker repairs are completed while you wait, and we repair every major brand – Apple, Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola, OnePlus, Oppo, Sony, Huawei and Xiaomi.

Battery replacement

If your phone dies early, shuts down in the cold or charges slowly, a battery replacement is usually the quickest fix. We replace batteries in phones, tablets, laptops and MacBooks with new, tested cells, and every battery replacement comes with a 12-month warranty. Our guide to battery health and when to replace explains what the numbers in your settings mean.

Laptop, MacBook and computer repair

Our laptop repair service covers cracked screens, failed keyboards and trackpads, worn batteries, broken hinges and charging ports on Windows laptops from HP, Lenovo, Acer, Asus and Microsoft Surface. MacBook repair includes MacBook Air and Pro screens, batteries, keyboards and logic-board work. For desktop PCs we offer computer repair, virus and malware removal, data recovery, SSD and RAM upgrades, and custom gaming PC builds. Laptop and computer repairs carry a 12-month warranty.

Tablet and iPad repair

We fix iPad screens, batteries and charging ports across every generation – iPad, iPad mini, iPad Air and iPad Pro – as well as Samsung Galaxy Tab and other Android tablets. Many tablet repairs are completed the same day; your store will confirm timing when you book.

Games console repair

From PlayStation repair – PS5 and PS4 HDMI ports, overheating, disc drives and controller stick drift – to Xbox repair and Nintendo Switch repair, our games console repair team gets you back in the game. We also build and upgrade custom gaming PCs in store.

Smartwatch and wearable repair

We repair smartwatches and fitness trackers, including Apple Watch and Samsung Galaxy Watch screens, batteries and buttons. Book online or bring your watch into your nearest store for a quote.

Water damage, charging port, camera and speaker repairs

Dropped your phone in water? Switch it off and read what to do with a water-damaged phone, then bring it to us for liquid-damage cleaning and board-level diagnostics. We also replace charging ports, cameras, microphones, speakers and back glass on all major phones.

Sell your device or buy pre-owned

Upgrading? We buy phones, tablets, laptops and consoles for cash in store, or you can put the value towards a device from our online store. Every device we sell has passed a 60-point quality check and comes with a two-year warranty and a free Device MOT every six months.

Frequently asked questions

Prices, turnaround times, warranties and parts – the questions our stores are asked most.

How much does a phone screen replacement cost?
It depends on the model and the type of screen (OLED or LCD). Use the booking tool at the top of this page to get an instant price for your exact phone, or read our guide to iPhone screen repair costs in the UK. Prices are upfront with no hidden fees, and every phone screen we fit comes with a lifetime warranty.

How long does a phone repair take? Can I get it done the same day?
Yes. Most phone screen repairs, battery replacements and common faults are completed the same day, often within 1–3 hours. More complex work such as motherboard or water-damage repair can take longer depending on diagnostics and parts availability.

Do you repair all phone brands?
Yes. We repair Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Motorola, OnePlus, Oppo, Sony, Huawei, Xiaomi and most other Android phones.

Do you also repair tablets, laptops, MacBooks and games consoles?
We do. Our stores repair iPads and Android tablets, Windows laptops and desktop PCs, MacBooks, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo Switch consoles, smartwatches and fitness trackers, drones and other electronics. We also build custom gaming PCs.

Do you repair smartwatches and other wearables?
Yes. We repair smartwatches and fitness trackers, including Apple Watch and Samsung Galaxy Watch, covering screens, batteries and buttons. Book online or ask your nearest store for a quote.

Do your repairs come with a warranty?
Yes. Phone screen repairs have a lifetime warranty, and most other repairs – batteries, charging ports, cameras, tablets, laptops and consoles – carry a 12-month warranty covering parts and labour. Board-level soldering repairs are covered for three months.

What parts do you use – are they genuine?
We use genuine and OEM-quality replacement parts. Genuine Apple parts are available for iPhone repairs, Samsung repairs use genuine Samsung parts and Google Pixel repairs use genuine Google parts. Every device is tested before it goes back to you.

Do I need to book a repair in advance?
Walk-ins are welcome at all 10 stores. Booking online is recommended, though, because it reserves your parts and secures the fastest turnaround.

What if I can't get to a store?
Besides in-store repair we offer a mail-in service, collection and drop-off, and a come-to-you doorstep service from selected stores. Choose the option that suits you when you book, or contact your nearest store.

Can you fix a water-damaged phone?
Often, yes. Switch the phone off, don't charge it and bring it to us as soon as possible for liquid-damage cleaning and diagnostics. Read what to do with a water-damaged phone first – we'll tell you what can be saved before any work starts.

Addresses, opening hours and phone numbers are on our store locator.

Do you buy phones and other devices?
Yes. Bring your old phone, tablet, laptop or console into any store for a cash offer, or check our trading price online. We also sell checked, pre-owned devices with a two-year warranty in our online store.
