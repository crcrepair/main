Why Catalyst?

Catalyst Skills Group brings together two specialist academies, Catalyst Care and Catalyst Digital, built on the experience of Alan Hester Associates. We work with employers to design apprenticeships around the roles they need to fill, not the other way round.

We support new starters taking their first step into work and experienced staff ready to lead. Many of our learners come to us without a traditional route into a career. We believe the right training, at the right time, changes everything.
