iPhone 11 Pro Max — big screen, triple camera

Together with the iPhone 11 Pro, the iPhone 11 Pro Max was the first iPhone with a triple-camera system and Apple's Super Retina XDR display, here on a big 6.5-inch screen. Inside is the A13 Bionic chip, and Apple still supports it with iOS 27.

Compared with the iPhone XS Max it lasts up to five hours longer on a charge, its screen is noticeably brighter, and it adds an ultra-wide camera and Night mode for low-light photos. The stainless steel frame, textured matte glass back and IP68 rating make it a tough, premium phone, and it comes with a charging cable as standard.

Before you buy — good to know

Lightning, not USB-C. It charges through Apple's Lightning port, so your existing Lightning cables and accessories fit. Standard Qi wireless chargers work, but there are no MagSafe magnets.

4G, not 5G. It runs on fast 4G LTE and Wi-Fi 6, which covers everyday use on every UK network. 5G arrived with the iPhone 12.

No Apple Intelligence. The phone is supported by iOS 27, but Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later, and some of the newest iOS features are limited on older models.

What makes the iPhone 11 Pro Max stand out

Triple camera system

Wide, Ultra Wide and Telephoto lenses, all 12MP, give you a 4x optical zoom range. Step back for a sweeping landscape or zoom in for a portrait without moving.

Night mode and Deep Fusion

Night mode switches on automatically in low light for brighter, cleaner photos without a flash. Deep Fusion brings out fine texture and detail in indoor and mid-light shots.

6.5-inch Super Retina XDR

A large OLED screen with deep blacks and up to 800 nits of brightness, rising to 1,200 nits for HDR video. It's easy to read outdoors and great for films and games.

Battery that lasts

Up to 20 hours of video playback, up to five hours more than the iPhone XS Max. Fast charging takes it to about 50% in around half an hour with an 18W adapter.

A13 Bionic chip

Apple's A13 Bionic keeps apps, photos and games running smoothly, and the phone is still supported by iOS 27 for security updates and new features.

Built to last

A surgical-grade stainless steel frame and textured matte glass back give it a premium feel. IP68 water resistance covers depths of up to 4 metres for 30 minutes.

Is the iPhone 11 Pro Max right for you?

It suits you if you want a big, bright screen and a genuinely good triple camera without paying for a newer model. It's a clear step up from an iPhone XS Max, X or 8 Plus, with better battery life, Night mode and the ultra-wide lens.

Does the iPhone 11 Pro Max still get iOS updates?
Yes. Apple supports the iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27. Some newer iOS features need more recent hardware, and Apple Intelligence is only available on the iPhone 15 Pro and later.

Does the iPhone 11 Pro Max support 5G?
No. It uses fast 4G LTE, which works on every UK network, and Wi-Fi 6 for quick home and office connections. For 5G you would need an iPhone 12 or later.

Can I charge it wirelessly?
Yes. It works with standard Qi wireless chargers. It has no MagSafe magnets, so magnetic MagSafe accessories won't attach. With the cable and an 18W or higher adapter it fast-charges to about 50% in around 30 minutes.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can use two numbers on one phone. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.
