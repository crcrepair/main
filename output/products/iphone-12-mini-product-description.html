iPhone 12 mini — the smallest 5G iPhone

The iPhone 12 mini fits a 5.4-inch Super Retina XDR screen into a phone that weighs just 133g and is smaller than an iPhone SE, so it sits comfortably in one hand and slips into any pocket. Inside is the same A14 Bionic chip as the full-size iPhone 12, and Apple supports it with iOS 27.

You get the same dual 12MP camera as the iPhone 12 too, with Night mode on both lenses and Dolby Vision HDR video. It was the first mini with 5G, MagSafe and a Ceramic Shield front, and it's the most affordable way into a small, modern iPhone.

Before you buy — good to know

Smaller battery. Up to 15 hours of video playback is fine for a normal day, but it's the shortest of the iPhone 12 range. If you watch a lot of video or play games, the iPhone 12 or 13 mini last longer.

Lightning, not USB-C. The iPhone 12 mini charges through Apple's Lightning port; USB-C arrived with the iPhone 15. MagSafe and Qi wireless chargers work as normal.

No Apple Intelligence or Crash Detection. Apple supports it with iOS 27, but Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later, and Crash Detection arrived with the iPhone 14.

What makes the iPhone 12 mini stand out

Smaller than an iPhone SE

At 131.5mm tall and 133g, it's easy to use with one thumb and disappears into a pocket, while the edge-to-edge OLED screen still gives you a 5.4-inch display.

A14 Bionic

The same chip as the full-size iPhone 12, so apps, games and photo editing run just as quickly on the mini. Apple supports it with iOS 27.

5G and MagSafe

The first small iPhone with 5G for fast downloads, and MagSafe chargers, cases and wallets that snap into place magnetically.

Dual 12MP camera with Night mode

A Wide camera with a bright ƒ/1.6 lens and an Ultra Wide for landscapes, with Night mode on both, Deep Fusion for detail and Dolby Vision HDR video.

Super Retina XDR OLED

A sharp 476 ppi OLED screen with true blacks and HDR up to 1,200 nits, in place of the SE's LCD, with Face ID instead of a Home button.

Ceramic Shield and IP68

A Ceramic Shield front with four times better drop performance than the iPhone 11, and IP68 water and dust resistance to 6 metres.

Is the iPhone 12 mini right for you?

It's the right choice if you want a genuinely small iPhone with a modern edge-to-edge screen, Face ID and 5G, at the lowest price for a mini. Coming from an iPhone SE, 8 or older, you'll notice the bigger, sharper screen in a smaller body, the faster chip and the much better camera.

If battery life matters most, the iPhone 12 has a bigger battery, and the iPhone 13 mini lasts two hours longer in the same size. What's the difference between the iPhone 12 mini and iPhone SE (2020)?
The 12 mini has 5G, an edge-to-edge 5.4-inch OLED screen with Face ID (the SE has a 4.7-inch LCD with a Home button and Touch ID), the faster A14 Bionic chip, a second Ultra Wide camera with Night mode, MagSafe and better water resistance. Despite the bigger screen, it's slightly smaller than the SE.

Should I choose the iPhone 12 mini or the iPhone 13 mini?
They're the same size. The 13 mini has the newer A15 chip, longer battery life of up to 17 hours of video against 15, Cinematic mode and sensor-shift stabilisation on the main camera, and it starts at 128GB. The 12 mini is the cheaper way to get a small 5G iPhone with the same screen size and MagSafe.

Is the iPhone 12 mini still supported by Apple?
Yes. Apple supports the iPhone 12 mini with iOS 27. Apple Intelligence isn't available on it, though, as it needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can use two numbers, and 5G works wherever your network offers it. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.
