iPhone 14 Pro Max — Dynamic Island, 48MP camera, longest battery

The iPhone 14 Pro Max was the top iPhone of 2022, and it introduced two things that defined the Pro line since: the Dynamic Island, which replaces the notch with a live area for alerts and activities, and the Always-On display, which shows the time and widgets while the screen is locked. The 6.7-inch ProMotion screen reaches 2,000 nits outdoors, and the A16 Bionic chip runs it all.

The camera is the first iPhone with a 48MP main sensor, which gives a 2x option at full quality and ProRAW at 48MP, alongside a 3x telephoto and an Ultra Wide with macro. Action mode steadies handheld video, and the battery lasts up to 29 hours of video, the longest of any iPhone at launch. Crash Detection and Emergency SOS via satellite add peace of mind.

Before you buy — good to know

Big and heavy. At 240g and 160.7mm tall it's the largest and heaviest iPhone 14. If you'd rather have the same cameras in a phone that's easier to use one-handed, the iPhone 14 Pro is 206g.

Lightning, not USB-C. The iPhone 14 Pro Max charges through Apple's Lightning port; USB-C arrived with the iPhone 15. MagSafe and Qi wireless chargers work as normal.

No Apple Intelligence. Apple supports the phone with iOS 27, but Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later. Photos are 12MP by default; 48MP needs ProRAW, and those files are large.

What makes the iPhone 14 Pro Max stand out

Up to 29 hours of video

The longest battery life of any iPhone at launch, six hours more than the iPhone 14 Pro, with fast charging to around 50% in 35 minutes using a 20W adapter.

Dynamic Island and Always-On

The Dynamic Island shows music, timers, calls and Live Activities without leaving your app, and the Always-On display keeps the time, widgets and wallpaper visible on the lock screen at 1Hz to save power.

48MP main camera

The quad-pixel sensor captures four times the detail for ProRAW, and crops to a 2x view at full quality, so you get four useful focal lengths: 0.5x, 1x, 2x and 3x.

6.7-inch ProMotion display at 2,000 nits

The brightest iPhone screen at launch, readable in direct sunlight, with a refresh rate that adapts from 1Hz to 120Hz.

Action mode and pro video

Action mode smooths out shake and bumps for steady handheld footage, Cinematic mode now records in 4K, and ProRes and Dolby Vision suit creators.

Crash Detection and satellite SOS

If you're in a severe car crash, the phone can detect it and call the emergency services. Where there's no mobile or Wi-Fi signal, Emergency SOS via satellite can still send a message for help.

Is the iPhone 14 Pro Max right for you?

It's the right choice if you want the biggest screen, the longest battery and the 48MP Pro camera at a pre-owned price, for video, games, photography and editing. Coming from an iPhone 11 Pro Max, 12 Pro Max or 13 Pro Max, you'll notice the Dynamic Island, the Always-On display, the brighter screen and the 2x option.

If you'd like the same cameras in a phone that's easier to use one-handed, the iPhone 14 Pro is the one to look at. For USB-C and Apple Intelligence, you'll need an iPhone 15 Pro or later, which also adds a 5x telephoto on the Pro Max. Not sure which suits you? What's the difference between the iPhone 14 Pro Max and iPhone 13 Pro Max?
The 14 Pro Max replaces the notch with the Dynamic Island, adds an Always-On display that's brighter outdoors (2,000 nits against 1,200), a 48MP main camera in place of 12MP with a 2x option, the faster A16 Bionic chip, Action mode video, Crash Detection and Emergency SOS via satellite. Battery life is up to 29 hours of video against 28. Both have the 3x telephoto and 120Hz ProMotion.

Should I choose the iPhone 14 Pro Max or the iPhone 14 Pro?
They share the same chip, cameras, Dynamic Island and Always-On display. The Pro Max has a larger 6.7-inch screen and the longest battery of the two, up to 29 hours of video against 23, but it's bigger and heavier at 240g against 206g. Pick the Max for screen and battery, the Pro for one-handed use.

What is the Dynamic Island?
The Dynamic Island is the pill-shaped area at the top of the screen that replaces the notch. It expands to show what's happening in the background: music, timers, directions, calls and Live Activities such as a food delivery, so you can glance or tap without leaving the app you're in.

Is the iPhone 14 Pro Max still supported by Apple?
Yes. Apple supports the iPhone 14 Pro Max with iOS 27. Apple Intelligence isn't available on it, though, as it needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later.
