iPhone 15 Pro Max — titanium design, 5x zoom

The iPhone 15 Pro Max is the largest iPhone 15, with a 6.7-inch ProMotion display and the first 5x telephoto camera ever fitted to an iPhone. Together with the iPhone 15 Pro, it was the first iPhone built around a titanium frame and a 3-nanometre chip, the A17 Pro. At 221g it is 19g lighter than the iPhone 14 Pro Max.

It charges over USB-C, can record ProRes video straight to an external drive, and swaps the mute switch for a customisable Action button. The battery gives up to 29 hours of video playback, the longest in the iPhone 15 range, and it supports Apple Intelligence.

Before you buy — good to know

USB-C, not Lightning. Older Lightning cables and accessories need Apple's USB-C to Lightning adapter. MagSafe cases, wallets and chargers work as normal.

No power adapter. Apple doesn't put one in the box. Any USB-C charger works, and a 20W or higher adapter gives you fast charging.

It's a big phone. The 6.7-inch screen and 221g weight suit two-handed use. If you'd rather use your phone one-handed, the iPhone 15 Pro has the same chip and main camera in a 6.1-inch body, with 3x zoom instead of 5x.

What makes the iPhone 15 Pro Max stand out

5x telephoto zoom

A tetraprism design folds the light path inside the phone to give a 120mm, 5x optical zoom. That means sharp close-ups of sport, wildlife or a school play from your seat. It's the only iPhone 15 with 5x zoom.

Titanium, and lighter

The aerospace-grade titanium frame cuts the weight to 221g, 19g less than the iPhone 14 Pro Max, which you notice on a phone this size. Ceramic Shield protects the front, with a textured matte glass back.

A17 Pro chip

Apple's first 3nm chip handles everyday tasks and heavy apps with ease. Its GPU is up to 20% faster than the A16 Bionic and adds hardware ray tracing, so console games such as Resident Evil Village and Assassin's Creed Mirage run on the phone itself.

Pro video over USB-C

Shoot ProRes and Log video for professional editing, and record 4K at 60fps ProRes directly to an external drive. With a USB 3 cable, files move to a computer at up to 10Gb/s.

Longest battery in the range

Up to 29 hours of video playback, more than any other iPhone 15. MagSafe and Qi2 wireless charging run at up to 15W, and the USB-C port charges everything else you carry.

Apple Intelligence

The iPhone 15 Pro Max runs Apple Intelligence, including Writing Tools, Clean Up in Photos and a more capable Siri. Features arrive with iOS updates.

Is the iPhone 15 Pro Max right for you?

It suits you if you want the biggest screen, the longest zoom and the longest battery life in the iPhone 15 range: for watching video, gaming, or photographing things you can't get close to. It's a natural upgrade from an older Pro Max, adding 5x zoom, USB-C and a lighter titanium build.

iPhone 15 Pro Max — frequently asked questions

Does the iPhone 15 Pro Max come with a charger?
Apple's own box includes a USB-C charge cable but no power adapter. Any USB-C charger will work. For fast charging (about 50% in around 30 minutes) you need a 20W or higher USB-C power adapter.

How good is the 5x zoom?
The telephoto camera gives true 5x optical zoom, the equivalent of a 120mm lens, so you can get close-up shots from across a room, a pitch or a stage without losing detail. Digital zoom goes up to 25x. The telephoto has its own 3D sensor-shift stabilisation, which keeps zoomed photos and video steady.

Does the iPhone 15 Pro Max support Apple Intelligence?
Yes. The iPhone 15 Pro Max and 15 Pro are the earliest iPhones that support Apple Intelligence. Features such as Writing Tools and Clean Up in Photos come with the latest version of iOS.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can run two numbers on one phone. 5G works wherever your network offers it. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.

What's the difference between the iPhone 15 Pro Max and 15 Pro?
The Pro Max has a bigger 6.7-inch display, a 5x telephoto camera (the 15 Pro has 3x) and a larger battery, with up to 29 hours of video playback against 23. Both share the A17 Pro chip, the 48MP main camera and the titanium design. The 15 Pro is smaller and lighter, at 187g against 221g.
