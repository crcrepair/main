iPhone 16 Plus — big screen, longest battery

The iPhone 16 Plus gives you everything in the iPhone 16 on a larger 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR screen, with a bigger battery to match: up to 27 hours of video playback. It's built for Apple Intelligence, with the A18 chip running Apple's AI features for writing, photo editing, image creation and a more helpful Siri.

Camera Control puts shooting, zooming and settings under your thumb, the 48MP Fusion camera adds a 2x telephoto option, and the Ultra Wide focuses close enough for macro shots. You also get the Dynamic Island, the Action button, 25W MagSafe charging and Wi-Fi 7.

Before you buy — good to know

It's a big phone. The 6.7-inch screen and 199g weight are great for films, games and reading, but it's best used with two hands. The iPhone 16 has the same features in a 6.1-inch size.

60Hz screen, no Always-On. 120Hz ProMotion and the Always-On display are only on the iPhone 16 Pro models.

2x zoom, no telephoto lens. The 2x option comes from the 48MP main sensor; 5x optical zoom is on the iPhone 16 Pro Max. Wired USB-C transfers also run at USB 2 speed.

What makes the iPhone 16 Plus stand out

Apple Intelligence

Writing Tools rewrite and summarise your text, Clean Up removes distractions from photos, Image Playground and Genmoji create images, and Siri understands more. New features arrive with iOS updates.

Camera Control

A touch-sensitive button that opens the camera, takes the shot and lets you slide to zoom or adjust exposure. It also launches Visual Intelligence to look things up through the camera.

48MP Fusion and macro

The main camera shoots 24MP or 48MP photos and a 2x telephoto crop. The Ultra Wide adds autofocus, so you can get crisp close-ups of flowers, food and fine detail.

6.7-inch Super Retina XDR

A big, bright OLED screen with the Dynamic Island, up to 2,000 nits outdoors and 1,600 nits for HDR. Ideal for films, games, maps and split-screen-style multitasking.

A18 chip

Faster than the iPhone 15's chip, with a 5-core GPU for smooth gaming and plenty of headroom for Apple Intelligence. Apple supports the phone with iOS 27.

27-hour battery

Up to 27 hours of video playback, five more than the iPhone 16. MagSafe and Qi2 chargers charge at up to 25W, and USB-C fast-charges with a 20W or higher adapter.

Is the iPhone 16 Plus right for you?

It suits you if you want a large screen and the longest battery life of the standard iPhone 16 models, plus Apple Intelligence and Camera Control. It's a natural upgrade from an older Plus or Max model, or from any iPhone that struggles to last the day.

iPhone 16 Plus — frequently asked questions

What comes with the iPhone 16?
It comes with a charging cable as standard. For a plug, screen protector, case or MagSafe charger, take a look at our phone accessories. The 15% discount on selected accessories comes off automatically in the basket.

What is Camera Control?
Camera Control is a touch-sensitive button on the side of the iPhone 16 Plus. Press it to open the camera, press again to take a photo, and slide your finger along it to zoom or change settings. It also opens Visual Intelligence, which tells you about whatever you point the camera at.

What's the difference between the iPhone 16 Plus and iPhone 16?
They share the same A18 chip, cameras, Camera Control and Apple Intelligence. The 16 Plus has a larger 6.7-inch screen (the iPhone 16 is 6.1-inch) and a bigger battery, with up to 27 hours of video playback against 22. It's heavier, at 199g against 170g.

What's the difference between the iPhone 16 Plus and iPhone 15 Plus?
The 16 Plus has the faster A18 chip and supports Apple Intelligence, which the 15 Plus doesn't. It adds Camera Control, the Action button, macro photos and spatial video, faster 25W MagSafe charging and Wi-Fi 7, and its battery lasts a little longer: up to 27 hours of video against 26.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can use two numbers, and 5G works wherever your network offers it. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.
