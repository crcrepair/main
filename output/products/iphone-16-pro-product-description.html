iPhone 16 Pro — bigger screen, 5x zoom, 4K 120fps

The iPhone 16 Pro grows the Pro screen to 6.3 inches with slimmer borders, and runs on the A18 Pro chip with full support for Apple Intelligence. The titanium frame keeps it strong, and the ProMotion display is smooth at up to 120Hz with an Always-On option.

Its camera system is a real step up: a 48MP Fusion main camera, a new 48MP Ultra Wide for detailed macro shots, and a 5x telephoto. Video reaches 4K at 120fps in Dolby Vision, and four studio-quality microphones capture clear sound. Camera Control, the Action button, Wi-Fi 7 and up to 27 hours of video playback complete the package.

Before you buy — good to know

4K, not 8K. Video tops out at 4K, at up to 120fps in Dolby Vision, which is smoother than most phones manage, but it doesn't record 8K.

A little heavier. At 199g it's 12g heavier than the iPhone 15 Pro, because of the larger screen and battery.

No power adapter. It comes with a charging cable. For 25W MagSafe charging you'll need a MagSafe charger and a 30W adapter.

What makes the iPhone 16 Pro stand out

48MP triple camera with 5x zoom

Fusion, Ultra Wide and Telephoto lenses cover 0.5x to 5x. The 48MP Ultra Wide captures detailed macro shots, and the 5x tetraprism telephoto, previously Pro Max only, gets you close from far away.

4K 120fps Dolby Vision

Record 4K at 120 frames per second for smooth slow motion you can adjust after filming. ProRes and Log recording, spatial video and Audio Mix suit creators and pros.

Apple Intelligence on A18 Pro

Writing Tools, Clean Up, Image Playground, Genmoji and a smarter Siri run on the A18 Pro, which also handles demanding games with hardware ray tracing.

Camera Control

Press to launch the camera and capture, slide to zoom or change exposure, and open Visual Intelligence to identify places, plants and more.

6.3-inch ProMotion display

A larger screen with the thinnest borders on any iPhone at launch, refresh rates up to 120Hz, an Always-On mode and brightness that drops to 1 nit for night-time reading.

Longer battery and fast USB 3

Up to 27 hours of video playback, four more than the iPhone 15 Pro. MagSafe charges at up to 25W, and USB-C moves files at up to 10Gb/s with a USB 3 cable.

Is the iPhone 16 Pro right for you?

It's the right choice if you want a Pro camera with 5x zoom and pro video in a size that's still comfortable to hold, plus Apple Intelligence and a smooth 120Hz screen. Coming from an iPhone 13 Pro, 14 Pro or older, you'll see a big jump in zoom, video and battery life.

If you want the largest screen and longest battery, the iPhone 16 Pro Max is the one to look at. If you don't need ProMotion or the telephoto, the iPhone 16 offers Apple Intelligence and Camera Control for less. Not sure which suits you? What's the difference between the iPhone 16 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro?
The 16 Pro has a slightly larger 6.3-inch screen (the 15 Pro is 6.1-inch), the faster A18 Pro chip, Camera Control, a 5x telephoto camera instead of 3x, a 48MP Ultra Wide, 4K video at 120fps, four studio-quality microphones and longer battery life: up to 27 hours of video against 23. Both support Apple Intelligence.

Is the iPhone 16 Pro worth it over the iPhone 16?
Choose the Pro if you want a 120Hz ProMotion screen with Always-On, the 5x telephoto camera, 4K 120fps and ProRes video, USB 3 transfers and a titanium build. The iPhone 16 shares Apple Intelligence and Camera Control but has a 60Hz screen and two cameras.

What is Camera Control?
Camera Control is a touch-sensitive button on the side of the iPhone 16 Pro. Press to open the camera and take photos, slide to zoom or adjust exposure, and use it to open Visual Intelligence for information about what's in front of you.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can use two numbers, and 5G works wherever your network offers it. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.
