iPhone 16 — Apple Intelligence and Camera Control

The iPhone 16 was built for Apple Intelligence. Its A18 chip runs Apple's AI features for writing, editing photos, creating images and a more helpful Siri, while staying fast for games and everyday apps. A new Camera Control button lets you shoot, zoom and adjust settings with a press or a swipe.

The 48MP Fusion camera takes sharp photos with a 2x telephoto option, and the Ultra Wide now focuses close enough for macro shots. You also get the Dynamic Island, the Action button, faster 25W MagSafe charging, Wi-Fi 7 and up to 22 hours of video playback.

Before you buy — good to know

60Hz screen, no Always-On. The display is bright and sharp, but 120Hz ProMotion and the Always-On display are only on the iPhone 16 Pro models.

2x zoom, no telephoto lens. The 2x option comes from the 48MP main sensor. For 5x optical zoom you'd need an iPhone 16 Pro.

USB 2 speeds. USB-C charges quickly, but wired file transfers run at USB 2 speed (up to 480Mb/s). For big video files, wireless transfer or the cloud is often quicker.

What makes the iPhone 16 stand out

Apple Intelligence

Writing Tools rewrite and summarise your text, Clean Up removes distractions from photos, Image Playground and Genmoji create images, and Siri understands more. New features arrive with iOS updates.

Camera Control

A touch-sensitive button that opens the camera, takes the shot and lets you slide to zoom or adjust exposure. It also launches Visual Intelligence to look things up through the camera.

48MP Fusion and macro

The main camera shoots 24MP or 48MP photos and a 2x telephoto crop. The Ultra Wide adds autofocus, so you can get crisp close-ups of flowers, food and fine detail.

Spatial video and Audio Mix

Record spatial photos and video to relive on Apple Vision Pro, and use Audio Mix to bring voices forward or cut background noise after you've filmed.

A18 chip

Faster than the iPhone 15's chip, with a 5-core GPU for smooth gaming and plenty of headroom for Apple Intelligence. Apple supports the phone with iOS 27.

Battery and faster MagSafe

Up to 22 hours of video playback. MagSafe and Qi2 chargers charge at up to 25W, and USB-C reaches about 50% in around 30 minutes with a 20W adapter.

Is the iPhone 16 right for you?

It suits you if you want Apple Intelligence and a modern, versatile camera in a standard-size iPhone. It's a big jump from an iPhone 12, 13 or 14, with Camera Control, the Action button, macro photos and longer battery life.

If you need a 120Hz screen, a telephoto lens or faster USB transfers, look at the iPhone 16 Pro. If you mainly want Apple Intelligence and the longest battery for less, the iPhone 16e is worth a look. Not sure which suits you? Ask in any of our stores and we'll talk you through the options.

iPhone 16 — frequently asked questions

What is Camera Control?
Camera Control is a touch-sensitive button on the side of the iPhone 16. Press it to open the camera, press again to take a photo, and slide your finger along it to zoom or change settings. It also opens Visual Intelligence, which tells you about whatever you point the camera at.

What's the difference between the iPhone 16 and iPhone 15?
The iPhone 16 has the faster A18 chip and supports Apple Intelligence, which the iPhone 15 doesn't. It also adds Camera Control, the Action button, macro photos and spatial video, faster 25W MagSafe charging, Wi-Fi 7 and longer battery life: up to 22 hours of video against 20.

Should I choose the iPhone 16 or the iPhone 16e?
Both run Apple Intelligence on an A18 chip. The iPhone 16 adds an ultra-wide camera with macro, the Dynamic Island, MagSafe and Camera Control. The 16e is cheaper and its battery lasts longer, but it has one rear camera and a notch.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can use two numbers, and 5G works wherever your network offers it. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.
