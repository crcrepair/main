iPhone XR — six colours, Face ID and all-day battery

The iPhone XR is the iPhone that brought Face ID and the edge-to-edge screen to a friendlier price, and it's still one of the best-value ways into a modern-looking iPhone. The 6.1-inch Liquid Retina LCD is bright and colour-accurate with True Tone, and the A12 Bionic chip keeps everyday apps, messaging and social media quick.

The single 12MP camera takes sharp photos in daylight with Smart HDR and Portrait mode, and records 4K video. Battery life is up to 16 hours of video, better than the iPhone X and XS it launched alongside. It comes in six colours, with a charging cable as standard.

Before you buy — good to know

No new iOS features. The iPhone XR's last major version is iOS 18. It still gets Apple's security updates and runs the everyday apps, but it won't get iOS 26 or 27 features. If staying current matters, the iPhone 11 is the same size and still supported.

LCD screen, single camera. The Liquid Retina LCD is sharp and accurate but lacks the deep blacks of OLED, and there's one rear camera: no Ultra Wide, no Night mode and no optical zoom.

Lightning and 4G. It charges through the Lightning port (Qi wireless works too, without MagSafe magnets) and connects over 4G LTE rather than 5G. Water resistance is IP67, to 1 metre.

What makes the iPhone XR stand out

Six bold colours

Choose from Black, White, Blue, Yellow, Coral or (PRODUCT)RED. The glass back and aluminium frame match the colour all the way round.

6.1-inch Liquid Retina display

An edge-to-edge LCD with True Tone, which adjusts the white balance to the room, and wide colour for accurate photos and video. The same size and resolution as the iPhone 11.

Face ID

Unlock the phone, sign in to apps and pay with Apple Pay by looking at it. The TrueDepth camera also takes Portrait mode selfies.

12MP camera with Smart HDR

The Wide camera has a bright ƒ/1.8 lens and optical image stabilisation. Smart HDR balances bright skies and shadows, and Portrait mode with Depth Control blurs the background behind people, adjustable after the shot.

All-day battery

Up to 16 hours of video playback, an hour and a half more than the iPhone X. With an 18W or higher adapter it fast-charges to about 50% in around 30 minutes, and Qi wireless charging works too.

A12 Bionic

Apple's first 7-nanometre chip handles apps, games and 4K video editing smoothly, with a Neural Engine for Face ID and photo processing.

Is the iPhone XR right for you?

It's a good choice if you want a big-screen iPhone with Face ID at the lowest price, for everyday use, as a first phone for a family member, or as a spare. The screen, battery and daylight camera are all still good, and it works on every UK network.

If you'd like Night mode, an Ultra Wide camera and the latest iOS features, the iPhone 11 costs a little more and is the same size. iPhone XR — frequently asked questions

What comes with the iPhone XR?
The phone comes with a charging cable as standard. For a plug, screen protector, case or wireless charger, browse our phone accessories. Your 15% discount on selected accessories is applied automatically in the basket.

Is the iPhone XR still supported by Apple?
Partly. The iPhone XR's last major version is iOS 18; it doesn't run iOS 26 or iOS 27, so it won't get new features. Apple has carried on releasing iOS 18 security updates for it, and all the everyday apps still work. If you want the latest iOS features, look at the iPhone 11 or newer.

What's the difference between the iPhone XR and iPhone X?
The XR has a bigger 6.1-inch screen (the X is 5.8-inch), the faster A12 Bionic chip, better battery life, six colours and a lower price. The X has a sharper OLED screen, a second telephoto camera and a stainless steel frame. Both have Face ID and wireless charging.

Should I choose the iPhone XR or the iPhone 11?
They're the same size with the same screen. The 11 adds an Ultra Wide camera, Night mode, the faster A13 chip, an hour more battery and, most importantly, it still runs the latest iOS. The XR is the cheaper option if you mainly want calls, messaging, social apps and a good camera in daylight.

Does the iPhone XR work with 5G?
No. It uses 4G LTE, which works on every UK network. Apple introduced 5G with the iPhone 12.
