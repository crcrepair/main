PlayStation 5 Pro — Sony's most powerful console

The PlayStation 5 Pro is the upgraded PS5, built to get more out of a 4K TV. Sony gave it a much larger graphics processor (67% more compute units than the standard PS5, and up to 45% faster rendering), memory that is 28% faster, and ray tracing that runs two to three times as fast. On top of that sits PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), Sony's AI-driven upscaling.

In practice, you get sharper pictures and steadier frame rates, especially in games labelled PS5 Pro Enhanced. Many of them let you keep ray tracing switched on without giving up a smooth 60fps, so you no longer have to choose between detail and smoothness. A 2TB SSD, Wi-Fi 7 and the DualSense controller complete the package, and it plays your existing PS5 games plus over 8,500 PS4 titles.

Before you buy — what the PS5 Pro doesn't include

No built-in disc drive. The PS5 Pro is a digital console. To play game discs or Blu-ray films, you need Sony's add-on Disc Drive, which is sold separately and attaches to the side of the console. If all your games are digital, you won't need it.

No vertical stand. The console sits flat as standard. Sony sells the vertical stand separately.

Your TV matters. For 120Hz modes and VRR you need a TV or monitor with HDMI 2.1. The Pro still runs games more smoothly on a 1080p TV, but most of the visual improvements are aimed at 4K screens.

What the PS5 Pro does better

PSSR AI upscaling

The console renders a game at a lower resolution, then uses AI to rebuild a sharp 4K image. You get close to native-4K detail without the frame-rate cost. A system setting can also improve PSSR image quality in supported games.

Faster ray tracing

Reflections, shadows and lighting are traced two to three times faster than on the standard PS5, so more games can offer ray tracing in their high frame-rate modes.

Higher, steadier frame rates

PS5 Pro Enhanced games add modes built for the Pro. Compatible games can run at 60fps, or up to 120fps on a 120Hz display, often with ray tracing still enabled.

2TB SSD as standard

Twice the storage of the original PS5, with the same very fast load times. There is room for more of your library before you need to delete anything, and you can expand it later.

DualSense controller

Haptic feedback and adaptive triggers let you feel the game: the tension of a bowstring, the kick of a weapon, or the surface under your feet.

Plays your existing library

All your PS5 games work, along with over 8,500 PS4 titles. Tempest 3D AudioTech adds positional sound through compatible headphones and TV speakers.

Is the PS5 Pro right for you?

The Pro makes most sense if you play on a 4K TV and image quality matters to you. Big open-world and action games, especially those labelled PS5 Pro Enhanced, show the clearest difference. If you were already short of storage, the 2TB drive is a genuine bonus.

If you play on a 1080p screen, mostly play older or less demanding games, or need a disc drive anyway, a standard PS5 may be better value. Not sure which suits you? Ask in any of our stores and we'll talk you through it.

PS5 Pro — frequently asked questions

Does the PS5 Pro have a disc drive?
No. The PS5 Pro is a digital console with no built-in drive. To play game discs or Blu-ray films you need Sony's add-on Disc Drive, which is sold separately and attaches to the side of the console. If you buy all your games digitally, you don't need it.

Will my PS5 and PS4 games work on the PS5 Pro?
Yes. It plays PS5 games and over 8,500 PS4 games. Titles labelled PS5 Pro Enhanced get extra modes built for the Pro's hardware, and some other games can run more smoothly too, although results vary from game to game.

Do I need a 4K TV for the PS5 Pro?
It works with any HDMI TV, but it is designed for 4K. On a 4K TV you see the full benefit of PSSR upscaling and the extra graphics power. For 120Hz modes and variable refresh rate (VRR) you need a TV or monitor that supports HDMI 2.1. On a 1080p screen, the gains are mainly smoother frame rates.

How much storage do I actually get?
The console has a 2TB SSD, but part of it is reserved for system software, so the space for games is a little under 2TB. You can add more with a compatible M.2 SSD in the expansion slot or with USB external storage.
