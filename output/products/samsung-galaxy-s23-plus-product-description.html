Galaxy S23+ — the big-screen Galaxy S23 with Snapdragon power

The Samsung Galaxy S23+ gives you a 6.6-inch 120Hz screen and a big 4,700mAh battery with 45W charging, in a flat-edged Armor Aluminum body with Gorilla Glass Victus 2 front and back. It runs the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy, the same chip as the S23 Ultra, which Samsung used in every region that year, UK included.

The triple camera pairs a 50MP main sensor with a 3x telephoto and an Ultra Wide, with Nightography for low light and 8K video. Galaxy AI arrived in a 2024 update, bringing Circle to Search, Live Translate and Generative Edit, and Samsung's four Android upgrades keep it current into 2028.

Before you buy — good to know

FHD+ screen, 48–120Hz. The screen is sharp and bright at 1,750 nits, but it's FHD+ rather than the QHD+ of the S23 Ultra and S24+, and its refresh rate adapts between 48Hz and 120Hz rather than down to 1Hz.

No charger in the box. It comes with a USB-C cable. For 45W fast charging you'll need a 45W USB-C PD adapter; wireless charging is 15W.

Galaxy AI arrived later, and updates end sooner. Galaxy AI came in 2024 with One UI 6.1, but some newer features are limited to the S24 series and later. Android 17 will be its last major upgrade, with security updates into 2028.

What makes the Galaxy S23+ stand out

Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy

A chip tuned for Samsung with higher clock speeds than the standard version, used in every region for the S23 series, for smooth gaming, 8K video and far better efficiency than the UK S22+'s Exynos.

6.6-inch 120Hz display

A flat Dynamic AMOLED 2X screen with 1,750 nits peak brightness for sunlight, an adaptive 48–120Hz refresh rate and an Always-On display, in the same size as the S24+.

4,700mAh battery, 45W charging

Comfortably lasts a full day, 200mAh more than the S22+, with 45W wired charging to around 65% in 30 minutes, 15W wireless charging and reverse wireless charging for earbuds or a watch.

50MP triple camera

A 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation, a 3x telephoto for portraits and detail, and a 120° Ultra Wide, with Nightography for low light and Astrophoto for the night sky.

Galaxy AI, added in 2024

Circle to Search finds anything on screen, Live Translate turns your calls into another language in real time, Note Assist tidies notes and Generative Edit moves or removes objects in photos.

Victus 2 and IP68

Gorilla Glass Victus 2 front and back, an Armor Aluminum frame and IP68 water and dust resistance to 1.5 metres, in a cleaner design without the S22's camera island.

Is the Galaxy S23+ right for you?

It's the right choice if you want a big-screen Samsung flagship with a Snapdragon chip, a strong battery and Galaxy AI at a price well below the S24+ and S25+. Coming from an S20+, S21+ or S22+, you'll notice the efficiency, the brighter screen and the AI features.

If you want a sharper QHD+ screen, 12GB of RAM and seven years of updates, the Galaxy S24+ has them, with the Exynos chip in the UK. For the S Pen, the 200MP camera and 10x zoom, look at the S23 Ultra. If you'd prefer something smaller, the Galaxy S23 has the same chip and cameras in a 6.1-inch phone. Not sure which suits you? What's the difference between the Galaxy S23+ and S22+?
The S23+ has the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy chip in every region, where the UK S22+ used Samsung's Exynos 2200, so it's faster and noticeably more efficient. The battery grew from 4,500 to 4,700mAh, storage starts at 256GB instead of 128GB, the glass is tougher Gorilla Glass Victus 2 and the camera island is gone for a cleaner design. The 6.6-inch screen, cameras and 45W charging are the same.

Should I choose the Galaxy S23+ or the S24+?
The S24+ has a sharper QHD+ screen, 12GB of RAM against 8GB, a slightly bigger battery, Galaxy AI from launch and seven years of updates against four. But in the UK the S24+ uses Samsung's Exynos 2400, while the S23+ has the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy, and the S23+ gained most Galaxy AI features in a 2024 update. The S23+ is the better-value choice if you don't need the sharper screen.

Does the Galaxy S23+ have Galaxy AI?
Yes, most of it. Galaxy AI launched on the S24 series and was added to the S23+ with One UI 6.1 in 2024: Circle to Search, Live Translate, Interpreter, Note Assist, Chat Assist and Generative Edit. Some newer AI features are limited to the S24 series and later.

Is the Galaxy S23+ still supported by Samsung?
Yes. Samsung promised four Android upgrades and five years of security updates from its 2023 launch, so it runs Android 16 with One UI 8, has Android 17 to come as its last major upgrade, and gets security updates into 2028.
