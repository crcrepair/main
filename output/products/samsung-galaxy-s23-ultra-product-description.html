Galaxy S23 Ultra — 200MP camera, 10x zoom and the built-in S Pen

The Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra is the flagship of the S23 range and the first Galaxy with a 200MP main camera. Alongside it sit a 12MP Ultra Wide and two telephoto lenses, a 3x and a 10x periscope, so you can shoot everything from wide landscapes to distant detail at optical quality, with Nightography for low light and 8K video.

The 6.8-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X screen runs at an adaptive 1–120Hz and reaches 1,750 nits, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy chip handles anything, and the S Pen is built into the phone for notes, sketches and signatures. A 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging, Gorilla Glass Victus 2 and IP68 water resistance complete a phone built for creators, gamers and heavy users.

Before you buy — good to know

Big and heavy. At 234g and 163.4mm tall it's one of the largest phones you can buy. The S Pen is part of the reason, so if you don't need a stylus, the Galaxy S23+ is lighter with the same chip.

No charger in the box. It comes with a USB-C cable. For 45W fast charging you'll need a 45W USB-C PD adapter; wireless charging is 15W.

Galaxy AI arrived later. The S23 Ultra gained Galaxy AI features with One UI 6.1 in 2024, but some newer AI features are limited to the S24 series and later. Android 17 will be its last major upgrade.

What makes the Galaxy S23 Ultra stand out

200MP main camera

The Adaptive Pixel sensor combines pixels for bright, detailed 12MP shots by default, or captures at 50MP or the full 200MP when you want to crop in. Nightography pulls detail out of low light.

3x and 10x optical zoom

Two telephoto lenses give true optical zoom at 3x and 10x, the periscope lens reaching 230mm-equivalent. Space Zoom goes to 100x, and both lenses are optically stabilised for sharper handheld shots.

Built-in S Pen

The stylus slots into the phone, so it's always to hand for notes, sketches, annotating screenshots and signing documents, and it works as a Bluetooth remote for the camera.

6.8-inch QHD+ 120Hz display

A Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel with 1,750 nits peak brightness for sunlight, an adaptive 1–120Hz refresh rate to save power, and flatter edges than the S22 Ultra for easier S Pen use.

Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy

A chip tuned for Samsung with higher clock speeds than the standard version, for smooth gaming, 8K video and all-day efficiency. Samsung DeX turns the phone into a desktop with a monitor.

5,000mAh battery, 45W charging

Comfortably lasts a full day of heavy use, with 45W wired charging to around 65% in 30 minutes, 15W wireless charging and reverse wireless charging for earbuds or a watch.

Is the Galaxy S23 Ultra right for you?

It's the right choice if you want the most capable Android phone of its year: the best zoom camera, a built-in stylus, a huge bright screen and a battery that lasts, at a pre-owned price well below the S24 and S25 Ultra. Coming from an S21 Ultra, S20 Ultra or a Note, you'll notice the 200MP camera, the brighter screen and the longer battery.

If you don't need the S Pen or the 10x zoom, the Galaxy S23+ has the same chip in a lighter phone. If you want titanium, a 5x telephoto and the newest Galaxy AI features, look at the Galaxy S24 Ultra or S25 Ultra. Not sure which suits you? What's the difference between the Galaxy S23 Ultra and S22 Ultra?
The S23 Ultra has a 200MP main camera in place of 108MP, the faster and more efficient Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy chip in every region (the UK S22 Ultra used Samsung's Exynos chip), tougher Gorilla Glass Victus 2, flatter screen edges and noticeably longer battery life from the same 5,000mAh cell. The screen, S Pen, zoom lenses and design are otherwise much the same.

Is the Galaxy S23 Ultra still supported by Samsung?
Yes. Samsung promised four Android upgrades and five years of security updates from its 2023 launch, so it runs Android 16 with One UI 8, has Android 17 to come as its last major upgrade, and gets security updates into 2028. Galaxy AI features such as Circle to Search and Live Translate were added in 2024.

Does the S Pen come with the phone?
Yes. The S Pen slots into the bottom of the phone, so it's always with you. Pull it out to take notes on the lock screen, annotate screenshots, sign documents or use it as a remote shutter for the camera.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can use two numbers, and 5G works wherever your network offers it. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.
