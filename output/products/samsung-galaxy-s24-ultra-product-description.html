Galaxy S24 Ultra — titanium, Galaxy AI and a 200MP camera

The Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra is the first Galaxy with a titanium frame and the first with Galaxy AI: Circle to Search, Live Translate on calls, Note Assist and Generative Edit for photos, most of it running on the phone. The flat 6.8-inch QHD+ screen uses anti-reflective Gorilla Armor glass and reaches 2,600 nits, the brightest Galaxy at launch.

The camera keeps the 200MP main sensor and adds a sharper 50MP 5x telephoto that gives optical-quality shots all the way to 10x. The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy chip, 12GB of RAM, a 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging and the built-in S Pen make it the complete flagship, with seven years of updates to come.

Before you buy — good to know

Big and heavy. At 232g and 162.3mm tall it's a large phone, only 2g lighter than the S23 Ultra despite the titanium. If you don't need a stylus, the Galaxy S24+ is lighter with the same Galaxy AI.

No charger in the box. It comes with a USB-C cable. For 45W fast charging you'll need a 45W USB-C PD adapter; wireless charging is 15W.

Some Galaxy AI features use the cloud. Most run on the phone, but a few need an internet connection and a Samsung account. Samsung has said some AI features may become paid after 2025.

What makes the Galaxy S24 Ultra stand out

Galaxy AI

Circle to Search finds anything on screen, Live Translate turns your calls into another language in real time, Note Assist tidies and summarises notes, and Generative Edit moves or removes objects in photos.

Titanium frame, flat screen

A titanium frame for strength and a premium feel, with a completely flat display and anti-reflective Gorilla Armor glass that cuts glare in sunlight and under office lights.

200MP camera with 50MP 5x zoom

The 200MP main sensor captures huge detail, and the new 50MP 5x telephoto gives sharp optical-quality shots from 5x to 10x, where the old 10x lens was weakest. Nightography handles low light.

6.8-inch QHD+ display at 2,600 nits

The brightest Galaxy screen at launch, with an adaptive 1–120Hz refresh rate, flat edges for easier S Pen use and an Always-On display.

Built-in S Pen

The stylus slots into the phone, so it's always to hand for notes, sketches and signatures, and it works as a Bluetooth remote for the camera.

Seven years of updates

Samsung promised seven years of Android upgrades and security updates from 2024, so a pre-owned S24 Ultra stays current until 2031, longer than any phone before it.

Is the Galaxy S24 Ultra right for you?

It's the right choice if you want the best Samsung camera, a built-in stylus, Galaxy AI and a phone that will keep getting updates for years, at a pre-owned price below the S25 Ultra. Coming from an S22 Ultra, S21 Ultra or a Note, you'll notice the brighter flat screen, the sharper zoom and the AI features.

If you don't need the S Pen or the 5x zoom, the Galaxy S24+ has the same Galaxy AI in a lighter phone. For the S23 Ultra, you get the same 200MP camera and S Pen for less but shorter update support and no titanium. Not sure which suits you? What's the difference between the Galaxy S24 Ultra and S23 Ultra?
The S24 Ultra has a titanium frame in place of aluminium, a completely flat screen with anti-reflective Gorilla Armor glass that's brighter at 2,600 nits, the faster Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy chip, 12GB of RAM on every model, Wi-Fi 7 and seven years of updates against four. The 10x periscope lens is replaced by a 50MP 5x lens that gives sharper results from 5x to 10x. Galaxy AI launched on the S24 and was later added to the S23.

What can Galaxy AI do on the S24 Ultra?
Circle to Search lets you circle anything on screen to search it. Live Translate translates phone calls in real time, Interpreter does the same face to face, Note Assist summarises and formats notes, Chat Assist adjusts the tone of messages, and Generative Edit moves or removes objects in photos and fills in the background. Most features run on the phone itself.

Is the Galaxy S24 Ultra still supported by Samsung?
Yes. Samsung promised seven years of Android upgrades and security updates from its 2024 launch, so it runs Android 16 with One UI 8 now and will keep getting new versions and security updates until 2031, longer than any Galaxy before it.
