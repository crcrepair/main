Galaxy S25 — the compact flagship with Galaxy AI

The Samsung Galaxy S25 is the small one of the range: a 6.2-inch 120Hz screen in a body that's 7.2mm thin and just 162g, so it's easy to use one-handed and light in a pocket. It runs the same Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy chip and 12GB of RAM as the S25 Ultra, a big step up from the Exynos chip in the UK S24.

The triple camera pairs a 50MP main sensor with a 3x telephoto and an Ultra Wide, with Nightography for low light and 8K video. Galaxy AI adds Now Brief, the Now Bar, actions across apps and Audio Eraser, and Samsung promises seven years of updates, so it stays current until 2032.

Before you buy — good to know

Aluminium, not titanium. The S25 has an Armor Aluminum frame with Gorilla Glass Victus 2. Titanium, the S Pen, the 200MP camera and the 5x zoom are S25 Ultra features.

Smaller battery, 25W charging. The 4,000mAh battery lasts a normal day but not as long as the S25+ (4,900mAh), and wired charging is 25W rather than 45W. No charger is included; it comes with a USB-C cable.

Some Galaxy AI features use the cloud. Most run on the phone, but a few need an internet connection and a Samsung account, and some Gemini features depend on Google's plans.

What makes the Galaxy S25 stand out

Compact and light

At 6.2 inches, 7.2mm and 162g it's one of the few small flagships left, comfortable one-handed and light in a pocket, with rounded corners, an Armor Aluminum frame and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 front and back.

Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy

The same 3nm flagship chip as the S25 Ultra, in every region this year, with a big jump in speed and efficiency over the Exynos in the UK S24, and 12GB of RAM for on-device AI.

Galaxy AI, now across apps

Ask for several things at once across apps, get a Now Brief summary of your day, see live activities in the Now Bar, and remove background noise from videos with Audio Eraser. Circle to Search and Live Translate are included.

50MP triple camera

A 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation, a 3x telephoto for portraits and detail, and a 120° Ultra Wide, with Nightography for low light and the ProVisual Engine for colour and detail.

6.2-inch 120Hz display

A flat Dynamic AMOLED 2X screen with 2,600 nits peak brightness for sunlight, an adaptive 1–120Hz refresh rate and an Always-On display.

Seven years of updates

Samsung promised seven years of Android upgrades and security updates from 2025, so a pre-owned S25 stays current until 2032, with IP68 water resistance and Wi-Fi 7 for the long haul.

Is the Galaxy S25 right for you?

It's the right choice if you want a genuinely compact phone with the newest Samsung chip, Galaxy AI and a proper telephoto camera, at the lowest price of the S25 range. Coming from an S21, S22, S23 or an A-series, you'll notice the lighter body, the much faster chip and the AI features.

Galaxy S25 — frequently asked questions

What comes with the Galaxy S25?

What's the difference between the Galaxy S25 and S24?
The S25 has the much faster Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy chip (the UK S24 used Samsung's Exynos 2400), 12GB of RAM in place of 8GB, a thinner and lighter body at 7.2mm and 162g, and the newer Galaxy AI features such as Now Brief, the Now Bar and actions across apps. The 6.2-inch screen, cameras, 4,000mAh battery and 25W charging are the same.

Should I choose the Galaxy S25 or the S25+?
They share the same chip, 12GB of RAM, cameras, Galaxy AI and seven years of updates. The S25+ has a larger 6.7-inch QHD+ screen, a bigger 4,900mAh battery and faster 45W charging. The S25 is the one to pick if you want a phone that's easy to use one-handed: 6.2 inches and 162g against 190g, and it's cheaper.

What can Galaxy AI do on the S25?
Now Brief gives a morning, midday and evening summary of your day. The Now Bar on the lock screen shows live activities. You can ask Gemini to do things across several apps in one request, Audio Eraser removes background noise from videos, and Circle to Search, Live Translate, Note Assist and Generative Edit are all included. Most features run on the phone.

Is the Galaxy S25 still supported by Samsung?
Yes. Samsung promised seven years of Android upgrades and security updates from its 2025 launch, so it runs Android 16 with One UI 8 now and will keep getting new versions and security updates until 2032.
