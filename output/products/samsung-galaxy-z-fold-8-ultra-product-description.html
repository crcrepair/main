Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra — the biggest Fold, with the best camera

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is Samsung's top foldable: an 8-inch inner screen with an anti-reflective coating and a shallower crease, a 6.5-inch cover screen that works as a normal phone, and a body just 4.1mm thin when open. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chip and 12GB of RAM run two or three apps at once without fuss.

It's the first Fold with a flagship-grade camera: a 200MP main sensor, a new 50MP Ultra Wide and a 3x telephoto. The 5,000mAh battery is the biggest in a Fold, charging at 45W, and Galaxy AI adds Now Nudge, My FanCam and Photo Assist, with seven years of updates to come.

Before you buy — good to know

Tall shape, heavier than the Fold8. The Ultra keeps the taller Fold7-style shape rather than the Fold8's wider one, and at 215g it's 14g heavier. If you want the lighter, pocket-friendlier Fold, the Galaxy Z Fold8 is the one.

IP48, not IP68. It's water-resistant to 1.5 metres, but only protected against particles over 1mm, so sand and fine dust are a risk for any folding phone. Treat it with a little more care than a normal phone.

No charger in the box, and no magnets for Qi2. It comes with a USB-C cable; 45W charging needs a 45W USB-C PD adapter. Wireless charging works, but magnetic Qi2 accessories need a case with a ring.

What makes the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra stand out

8-inch anti-reflective screen

The biggest screen on a Galaxy phone, with a new anti-reflective coating that cuts glare, up to 3,000 nits for sunlight, a 1–120Hz refresh rate and a shallower crease than the Fold7.

200MP camera with 3x zoom

The same 200MP main sensor as the Galaxy S Ultra phones, a 50MP Ultra Wide that's four times the resolution of the Fold7's, and a 10MP 3x telephoto for portraits and detail.

4.1mm unfolded

Thinner open than any Fold before it, 8.9mm closed, with a dual-rail hinge built for less flex and a flatter screen, and Flex Mode to stand it half-open for video calls and photos.

5,000mAh battery

The biggest battery in a Fold, with 45W wired charging, wireless charging and reverse wireless charging for earbuds or a watch, and the efficiency of the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5.

Multitasking on a tablet-size screen

Run up to three apps side by side, drag content between them, and let Now Nudge suggest a split-screen pairing from what you're doing, with a 6.5-inch cover screen for quick tasks closed.

Seven years of updates

Launched on Android 17 with One UI 9, with Android upgrades and security updates promised until 2033, so a pre-owned Fold8 Ultra stays current for years.

Is the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra right for you?

It's the right choice if you want the biggest screen you can fold into a pocket, a camera that matches a flagship phone, and the longest battery life of any Fold, at a pre-owned price well below new. Coming from a Fold5, Fold6 or Fold7, you'll notice the brighter anti-reflective screen, the Ultra Wide camera and the battery.

If you'd rather have a lighter, wider foldable and don't need the telephoto, the Galaxy Z Fold8 costs less. If you want a stylus, the Galaxy S26 Ultra has one built in, and its 5x zoom beats the Fold's 3x. Not sure which suits you? Ask in any of our stores and we'll talk you through the options.

What's the difference between the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra and Z Fold8?
The Ultra has a bigger 8-inch inner screen with an anti-reflective coating (the Fold8 is 7.6-inch), a 6.5-inch cover screen against 5.5-inch, a 200MP main camera with a 3x telephoto in place of the Fold8's two 50MP cameras, a 5,000mAh battery against 4,800mAh, and it's thinner unfolded at 4.1mm. It's also heavier at 215g against 201g and costs more. Both have the same chip, software and seven years of updates.

What's the difference between the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra and Z Fold7?
The Fold8 Ultra is the direct successor to the Fold7's slot: it keeps the tall 8-inch shape and the 200MP main camera, and adds a 50MP Ultra Wide in place of 12MP, an anti-reflective inner screen, a shallower crease, the newer Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chip, a bigger 5,000mAh battery and a slimmer, lighter body. It launched on Android 17 with One UI 9.

How good is the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra camera?
It's the best camera Samsung has put in a foldable: the same 200MP main sensor as the Galaxy S Ultra phones, a 50MP Ultra Wide and a 10MP 3x telephoto. It's a step behind the S26 Ultra's 5x zoom, and the telephoto gets grainy past 3x, but for everyday photos, portraits and video it's a proper flagship camera.

Is the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra waterproof?
It's rated IP48: water-resistant to 1.5 metres for 30 minutes, and protected against solid objects over 1mm, but not fully dust-sealed, so keep it out of sand and pockets full of grit. The new dual-rail hinge is designed for a shallower crease and less flex.
