iPhone 15 Pro — titanium design, Pro performance

The iPhone 15 Pro was the first iPhone made with a titanium frame and the first with a 3-nanometre chip, the A17 Pro. At 187g it is 19g lighter than the iPhone 14 Pro, yet it keeps everything that makes a Pro: a 6.1-inch ProMotion display, a 48MP Pro camera system with 3x optical zoom, and IP68 water and dust resistance.

It was also one of the first iPhones with USB-C, so one cable can charge your phone, iPad and Mac. A customisable Action button replaces the old mute switch, the battery lasts up to 23 hours of video playback, and it is one of the earliest iPhones to support Apple Intelligence.

Before you buy — good to know

USB-C, not Lightning. Older Lightning cables and accessories won't plug in without Apple's USB-C to Lightning adapter. MagSafe cases, wallets and chargers work as normal.

No power adapter. Apple doesn't include one in the box. Any USB-C charger works, and a 20W or higher adapter gives you fast charging.

3x zoom, not 5x. The 5x telephoto camera is only on the larger iPhone 15 Pro Max. The iPhone 15 Pro has a 3x telephoto, plus a 2x option from the 48MP main camera.

What makes the iPhone 15 Pro stand out

Titanium design

The aerospace-grade titanium frame is strong and light, so the phone is comfortable to hold for long periods. The front is protected by Ceramic Shield, with a textured matte glass back.

A17 Pro chip

Apple's first 3nm chip is fast and efficient. Its GPU is up to 20% faster than the previous generation and adds hardware-accelerated ray tracing, enough to run console titles such as Resident Evil Village and Assassin's Creed Mirage.

48MP Pro camera system

The 48MP main camera takes detailed 24MP photos by default and offers a sharp 2x option. Add the 12MP Ultra Wide and the 3x telephoto and you have a range of focal lengths, from landscapes to portraits. Video includes 4K at 60fps, ProRes and Log.

Action button

The old mute switch is now a programmable button. Set it to silent mode, the camera, the torch, a voice memo, translation, a Focus mode or your own Shortcut.

USB-C with USB 3 speeds

Charge your iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same cable. With a USB 3 cable, files transfer at up to 10Gb/s, which helps when you move large ProRes videos to a computer.

Apple Intelligence

The iPhone 15 Pro runs Apple Intelligence, including Writing Tools, Clean Up in Photos and a more capable Siri. Features arrive with iOS updates.

Is the iPhone 15 Pro right for you?

It suits you if you want a Pro camera, a titanium build and Apple Intelligence in a size that fits easily in one hand. It's also a natural step up from an iPhone 12, 13 or 14, with a noticeably better camera and the convenience of USB-C.

iPhone 15 Pro — frequently asked questions

Does the iPhone 15 Pro come with a charger?
Apple's own box includes a USB-C charge cable but no power adapter. Any USB-C charger will work. For fast charging (about 50% in around 30 minutes) you need a 20W or higher USB-C power adapter.

Will my old Lightning cables and accessories work?
Not directly. The iPhone 15 Pro uses USB-C, so Lightning cables won't plug in. Apple's USB-C to Lightning adapter works for many Lightning accessories. MagSafe cases, wallets and chargers all work as normal.

Does the iPhone 15 Pro support Apple Intelligence?
Yes. The iPhone 15 Pro and 15 Pro Max are the earliest iPhones that support Apple Intelligence. Features such as Writing Tools and Clean Up in Photos come with the latest version of iOS.

Will it work with my SIM and network?
UK models take a physical nano-SIM and also support eSIM, so you can use two numbers. The phone supports 5G where your network offers it. Ask us if you'd like to confirm the network status of a specific handset.

What's the difference between the iPhone 15 Pro and 15 Pro Max?
The Pro Max has a larger 6.7-inch display, a 5x telephoto camera instead of 3x, and a bigger battery (up to 29 hours of video playback against 23). The iPhone 15 Pro has the same A17 Pro chip, main camera and titanium design in a smaller, lighter body.
